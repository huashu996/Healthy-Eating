# Writing evidence-based meal explanations

This project guide describes how to explain a proposed meal without confusing a plan with an eaten meal or a nutrition estimate with medical advice. It is not a second Skill, a clinical guideline, or an automatic research service.

## Required reasoning chain

For each meaningful claim, distinguish:

1. **Food and portion:** what the proposed item actually is, including preparation and the quantity basis.
2. **Nutrition evidence:** matching product-label revision or traceable food-composition reference, units, calculation, and uncertainty.
3. **Purpose:** a bounded explanation of how that evidence relates to the person's stated goal or the meal's structure.
4. **Personal relevance:** only current, confirmed profile facts, preferences, restrictions, target date, and activity context. Unknown facts must stay unknown.
5. **Source and limits:** preserve the relevant source/version and explain what was not measured or verified.

A source about a nutrient does not by itself prove that a particular dish contains enough of it. Do not infer micronutrient adequacy, deficiency, disease prevention, or treatment from incomplete food records. Do not conflate eating fish with taking fish-oil supplements, and do not recommend duplicate supplements without checking relevant information and professional guidance.

## Review checklist

- Is the text about a proposal, actual intake, or a reference? Label it correctly.
- Do claimed nutrients correspond to documented foods, portions, and sources rather than an allocated budget?
- Are estimates explicitly identified, with important gaps retained?
- Does the explanation avoid diagnosing a condition or presenting software scores as clinical findings?
- Are health claims supported by the cited source, rather than extrapolated beyond it?
- Has the explanation been checked by a person or separately authorized integration? Software validation alone is not an independent scientific review.

When references are needed, consult current, relevant authoritative guidance and preserve its specific citation. This repository does not fetch or verify new research automatically.

An explanation-only revision should change explanation text and audit history, not silently recalculate intake, portions, targets, menu identity, or historical scores. Use the actual revision interface and current version checks.

## 中文摘要

说明应串联“实际菜式与份量 → 可追溯营养来源 → 有边界的用途 → 已确认的个体资料 → 来源与局限”。计划不冒充已吃，估算不冒充测量，缺失微量营养资料不判达标或缺乏。解释修订只调整说明并留下审计，不偷偷重算菜单、目标或历史评分。软件不会自动检索文献或完成独立科学核验。
